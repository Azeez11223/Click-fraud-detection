# Model card

AdShield's bundled and demo data are synthetic. Neither establishes detection quality on real ad traffic. The demo seed makes repeated headless traffic visible in the dashboard; its stored classifications are detector outputs and are not independent ground-truth labels. No attack detection rates or real-traffic performance metrics are claimed. Regenerate the synthetic training set with `python data/generate_dataset.py` and train on its fixed, time-ordered splits with `python -m ml.train`. Do not use this model alone to make high-impact decisions.

The offline trainer records the input data SHA-256, feature names, parameters, selected threshold, and holdout metrics in `models/metadata.json`. The serving feature contract includes counts, timing, and basic user-agent flags. Online score, decision, reason codes, and model version are persisted for predictions and event ingestion.

## Validation boundary

The project has not been validated against real advertiser traffic, an independently labelled click-fraud benchmark, or ad-platform billing outcomes. The synthetic dataset's labels are generated by the project and can encode the same patterns used by its rules/features; scores and training metrics therefore should not be interpreted as real-world effectiveness. The dataset is for illustrating the pipeline and local testing.

## Known failure modes

- Shared NATs, schools, offices, and mobile carrier gateways can make legitimate users appear as repeated-IP bursts.
- Legitimate VPN and proxy users can look unusual; no network intelligence feed is enabled by default.
- Low-and-slow abuse, rotating IPs, new automation frameworks, and sophisticated browser automation may evade the current simple rules and features.
- Sparse campaign history makes frequency and threshold estimates unstable. User-entered conversion assumptions may not match actual outcomes.
- User-agent strings are self-reported and can be changed or forged.

Review flagged events and treat all cost outputs as estimates. A decision is not proof of fraud or a guarantee of an advertising-platform refund.
